## Description

[h1]Piloting Lattice[/h1]

Piloting Lattice is a data-only Caves of Qud mod for Caves of Qud Modding Jam 5: Burgeoning.

Grow a garden with [b]Burgeoning[/b]. Sometimes, a passive chimeric vine rover grows among the plants. Use [b]Domination[/b] to climb inside the empty, low-ego chassis and pilot a purpose-built plant body while your original body lies dormant.

[h2]Features[/h2]

[list]
[*]Adds five tiers of passive vine-rover bodies to Burgeoning plant summoning tables.
[*]Uses real mechanics: custom vine anatomies, the Chimera morphotype mutation, natural weapons, Photosynthetic Skin, and darkvision.
[*]Rovers are good bodies and bad minds: strong physical stats, very low Ego, low mental defenses.
[*]Undominated rovers are quiet and defensive rather than autonomous bodyguards.
[*]Data-only: XML/JSON only, no C#, Harmony, or DLLs.
[/list]

[h2]How to use[/h2]

[list=1]
[*]Enable the mod.
[*]Play a character with Burgeoning and Domination.
[*]Use Burgeoning until a vine rover appears.
[*]Stand adjacent to the rover and Dominate it.
[*]Pilot the plant-chimera chassis.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]

This mod merges entries into [code]PlantSummoning1[/code] through [code]PlantSummoning9[/code]. It should be compatible with most data-only mods, but mods that replace the same population tables may affect rover spawn rates.
