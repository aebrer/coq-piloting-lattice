## Page body

Piloting Lattice is a data-only Caves of Qud mod for Caves of Qud Modding Jam 5: Burgeoning.

It adds passive chimeric vine rovers to Burgeoning's plant summoning tables. Grow a garden, find a hollow low-ego vine chassis among the plants, then use Domination to climb inside and pilot it while your original body lies dormant.

### What it adds

- Five tiers of mobile vine-rover bodies, from twitching rootlings to crowned trellis husks.
- Real chimeric plant mechanics: custom anatomies, the Chimera morphotype mutation, natural weapons, Photosynthetic Skin, and darkvision.
- Passive default behavior: rovers are meant to be bodies, not free autonomous bodyguards.
- Burgeoning integration across PlantSummoning1 through PlantSummoning9.
- No scripts, Harmony patches, or DLLs.

### How to play with it

1. Enable the mod in Caves of Qud.
2. Make or find a character with Burgeoning and Domination.
3. Use Burgeoning to grow plants.
4. When a vine rover appears, stand adjacent and Dominate it.
5. Go do plant-chimera crimes while your original body naps in the garden.

Undominated rovers are intentionally quiet and defensive. They are not meant to replace companions or ordinary summons; they are chassis waiting for a pilot.

### Compatibility

Piloting Lattice is data-only XML/JSON and should be broadly compatible with other mods unless they heavily replace the same Burgeoning plant summoning tables.

Built for the current Caves of Qud 1.0 stable line used by Caves of Qud Modding Jam 5.
